Daily Post Reminders

SMS program information

A once-a-day text reminder to post the day's scheduled video. This page explains the program. If you are enrolled, your own reminder link arrives in the text message itself.

  • Program: Sylva's Blessing — Daily Post Reminders, operated by Timothy Lovely, sole proprietor, at sylvasblessing.com.
  • Message frequency: about 1 message per day — roughly 30 messages per month. Frequency may vary.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To opt out: reply STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP.
  • Customer care: blovely1338@gmail.com
  • Terms & Conditions: sylvasblessing.com/terms
  • Privacy Policy: sylvasblessing.com/privacy

What the messages say

Each reminder names how many videos are queued for the day and carries a private link to that day's list. The link is personal to the recipient and is not published here.

Who receives these

These are internal operational reminders for the person who runs the publishing queue. They are not marketing, and the list is not shared with anyone.

How to sign up

Enrollment is at sylvasblessing.com/sms-opt-in. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Questions about the text program: blovely1338@gmail.com. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them.
